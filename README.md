# 🌲 XR Meditation — Escape the Office. Embrace the Forest.

Project website for **XR Meditation** — a research project exploring **persuasive system design in VR meditation**, part of an MSc thesis in Information Processing Science (Software Engineering) at the **University of Oulu** (OASIS Research Unit).

🔗 **Live site:** [shahriariqbal.com/XRMeditation](https://shahriariqbal.com/XRMeditation/)

## 📄 Research

- **Persuasive system design for VR meditation: A design science approach to enhancing cognitive absorption** — *Iqbal, Mattila & Guadagno (2026)*, BCSS 2026 · [CEUR Vol-4197](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4197/Paper4.pdf)
- **Toward a conceptual model for VR forest meditation: Challenges and future directions** — *Mattila, Iqbal & Karppinen (2026)*, ABC 2026 · position paper

🏅 Funded by an ITEE scholarship for XR & metaverse Master's thesis work.
